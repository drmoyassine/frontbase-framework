## North star

Frontbase is a reusable, self-hostable app builder. An owner connects their database and file storage, installs a configurable education-directory template, and edits and publishes it through `/frontbase-admin` without changing framework source. USA is the migration proof; a separate country deployment is the reuse proof. Each self-host deployment serves its own site/application through its own engine and state.

Supabase supplies reviewed current institution/program content and relationships. WordPress supplies original public URL identity and evidence of missing records. Missing optional covers disappear cleanly; content enrichment is a separate pass.

The Municipal Ransomware Epidemic

Centralized city IT is the #1 target. When it falls, everything — from 911 to water billing — goes with it.

AlertOctagon

City of Atlanta (March 2018)

SamSam ransomware crippled centralized city IT. Utility payments, parking, and court services knocked offline. Employees reverted to pen and paper for weeks. Police dashcam footage permanently destroyed. Total cost: $17 million — for a $51,000 ransom they refused to pay.

Lock

City of Baltimore (May 2019)

RobbinHood ransomware exploited outdated, centralized systems. City email, phone lines, and online billing offline for months. Real estate transactions halted citywide. Employees used personal Gmail for official business. Total cost: $18.2 million.

ShieldAlert

City of Dallas (May 2023)

Royal ransomware group infiltrated systems a full month before encrypting. Police and Fire dispatch (CAD) systems disrupted. 1.169 TB of data exfiltrated. 30,000+ individuals' PII compromised including SSNs and health records. $8.5M budget for recovery plus $4M for new detection systems.

The 911 Fragility Crisis

90% of US emergency communication centers experienced at least one outage in the past year. Many still run on decades-old analog systems. The CrowdStrike July 2024 incident forced 911 dispatch centers across multiple states to revert to paper-based manual dispatch. NG911 modernization requires $15+ billion.

Departmental Autonomy
